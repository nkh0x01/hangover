# Ride Safety Checklist

> Phase 2.2 deliverable. Mandatory pre-ride + in-ride safety items for
> every Hangover Driver. Read aloud during training. Printed on the
> back of the Day-1 quick reference card. Posted at the depot.
>
> No exceptions during pilot. A violation = strike. Repeated
> violations = off-board.

## Pre-shift (every time you go online)

- [ ] Helmet on (yours). DOT or ECE certified. No visible cracks.
- [ ] Reflective vest on. Daytime AND nighttime — improves
  visibility in city traffic.
- [ ] Passenger helmet in the cargo box, clean, dry, no cracks.
- [ ] Phone fully charged or charging on a holder within thumb reach.
  Mounted so you don't have to look down for more than 1 second.
- [ ] Fuel ≥ ⅓ tank.
- [ ] Brakes tested — squeeze each at 5 km/h before pulling onto
  the road.
- [ ] Both indicators tested.
- [ ] Headlight + brake light tested.
- [ ] Mirrors adjusted.
- [ ] Tire pressure visually checked; no obvious flats.
- [ ] No alcohol in your system. **Zero tolerance**.
- [ ] Not on medication that warns against driving.
- [ ] You feel alert. If you didn't sleep — go home.

## Pre-pickup

When you tap **Arriving** and again at **Arrived**:

- [ ] Park where the rider can safely step on — no live lane.
  Pull over fully.
- [ ] Engine off when waiting.
- [ ] Confirm passenger identity by name or app screen. Don't pick
  up someone who claims to be the rider without app confirmation.
- [ ] Hand them the passenger helmet. Make sure it's fastened.
  No fastened helmet = no trip.

## During the ride

- [ ] One passenger only. **No exceptions**. Two passengers on a
  scooter is a safety violation and an insurance void.
- [ ] No passengers under **12 years old**. Children's heads are too
  small for adult helmets; spinal injuries in falls are severe.
- [ ] No riding while holding the phone in your hand. The mount is
  there for a reason.
- [ ] No headphones / earbuds. You need to hear traffic.
- [ ] Speed limit + 0. Speeding endangers the rider and burns through
  the brake pads on a scooter much faster than a car.
- [ ] Predictable lane discipline. Lane-splitting only at < 30 km/h
  and only when traffic is stopped.
- [ ] No tight following — at least 2 seconds gap behind cars.
- [ ] Indicate every turn. Especially on the rear, where cars behind
  you often can't read your road position.

## Weather rules

| Conditions                            | Action            |
|----------------------------------------|-------------------|
| Light rain                            | OK, reduce speed by 20% |
| Heavy rain / standing water           | Pause and shelter. Resume when visibility > 50 m. |
| Strong wind > 50 km/h                 | Pause                |
| Fog visibility < 50 m                 | Pause                |
| Ice / black ice                       | Off the road. Go home. |
| First snow of the season              | Off the road for the day. |

Pause = tap **Offline**, find a sheltered spot. Resume when safe.

## Distractions

- [ ] Phone calls only on speakerphone via the helmet's intercom or
  the phone's built-in speaker. Hands stay on the bars.
- [ ] Do NOT eat, smoke, vape, or drink while moving.
- [ ] Do NOT interact with the app while moving except to tap a
  state transition at a stop.

## If something goes wrong

### Mechanical failure
1. Pull over safely.
2. Tap **Cancel ride** → reason "mechanical".
3. Help the passenger book another ride or arrange transport.
4. Call the depot. We'll arrange recovery if needed.

### Passenger emergency
1. Pull over. Engine off.
2. Call 112 if it's an injury or medical episode.
3. Stay with the passenger until help arrives.
4. Call our hotline immediately after.

### Crash / collision

**Priority order**:

1. **You and passenger safety first**. Move to the curb only if
   safely possible.
2. **Call 112**. Don't try to handle it alone.
3. **Don't move the vehicles** until the police arrive (Georgian law).
4. **Photograph** the scene — both vehicles, plates, road context, any
   injuries that allow photography.
5. **Exchange details** with the other party — license, insurance,
   plate.
6. **Call our hotline** — we'll dispatch an investigator and notify
   insurance.
7. **Cooperate fully** with the police report.
8. Take yourself **offline** for the rest of the day. We'll cover
   the lost earnings on the next payout.

### Aggressive or unsafe passenger
1. Pull over safely.
2. Politely end the ride. Refund the fare via the app.
3. Photograph the passenger and the plate / scene if you can do so
   without provoking them.
4. Call our hotline. Don't drive home. We'll dispatch backup if
   necessary.

### Suspicion of theft or fraud
- DO NOT confront. Complete the ride, then call us.

## Passenger consent rules

You may decline (with no penalty) to start a ride if:

- The passenger is too intoxicated to balance on the scooter.
- The passenger refuses to wear the helmet.
- The passenger has a child under 12 with them.
- The passenger has a large piece of luggage that won't fit safely.
- The passenger appears unwell to a degree that they may not survive
  the trip safely.

In each case: tap **Cancel ride** → reason "rider safety". Notify
support so they can follow up with the rider.

## End-of-shift

- [ ] Tap **Offline** before heading home.
- [ ] Settle any cash on hand against the day's earnings card.
- [ ] Charge the phone overnight.
- [ ] Clean the passenger helmet. Inspect for cracks.
- [ ] If you noticed any vehicle issue → log it on the maintenance
  sheet at the depot. Don't go online tomorrow until it's resolved.

## Cadence

Daily: take 30 seconds before going online to skim the pre-shift list.

Weekly: 5-min "tool-box" call with Ops where we review the week's
near-misses + reinforce one item from this list.

Monthly: refresher training session at the depot. 30 minutes,
mandatory.
