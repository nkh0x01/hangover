# Driver Training Guide

> Phase 2.2 deliverable. Script the trainer reads (literally, the
> first time) while the candidate driver follows along on their
> phone. Designed to take **~30 minutes** start to finish in a quiet
> classroom-style setting.

Outputs of this session: the driver knows the lifecycle, has done
one ride under supervision, and has the trainer's sign-off on
`driver-approval-checklist.md`.

## Materials

- Printed copy of this guide for the trainer.
- Printed driver "Day-1 quick reference" card for the driver
  (laminated, see appendix).
- Two phones with the Hangover Driver app installed (trainer + driver).
- One phone with the Hangover Customer app installed (Ops shadow rider).
- A test route ~2 km in length with a clear pickup point.
- Bottled water, helmet, vest.

## Script

### 0. Welcome (2 min)

> "Welcome to Hangover. We're going to walk you through how the app
> works in three parts: how a ride begins, how a ride ends, and what
> happens when something goes wrong. By the end you'll have done
> one ride yourself with my help."

Trainer: open the app on your phone. Show the home screen.

### 1. Going online (3 min)

> "On the home screen there's a big toggle that says **Offline** /
> **Online**. When it's grey, you can't get rides. When you tap it
> and it turns green, the app starts looking for nearby riders for
> you."

Trainer demonstrates:

1. Tap the toggle. Watch it turn green.
2. Confirm the location permission upgrade prompt that appears.
   Explain that "Always allow" lets the app keep your position
   fresh while the screen is off — otherwise the app would lose
   you between rides.
3. Driver does the same on their phone.

**Key point**: location and online status are completely separate.
Going offline immediately stops dispatching offers to you — you can
finish whatever you were doing and head home.

### 2. Receiving an offer (4 min)

> "When a rider books a ride near you, your phone will buzz, ring,
> AND show a card with the pickup point, drop-off point, and fare.
> You have **15 seconds** to accept. If you don't, the offer goes to
> another driver."

Trainer phones the Ops dispatcher: "Send a test offer to me." The
Ops shadow rider books a ride from inside the depot to a corner 800 m
away. The trainer's phone wakes with a high-priority push.

> "Notice the timer at the bottom. Notice the fare estimate at the
> top. Notice the pickup distance from where you are now."

Trainer accepts.

> "Now the app routes you to the pickup. The rider can see your
> position the entire time. The rider can call you — there's a phone
> icon on their screen — and you can call them by tapping the rider
> card."

Demonstrate the call button (don't actually dial).

### 3. Picking up the rider (3 min)

Trainer drives to the pickup, taps **Arriving** when 1-2 min away,
then **Arrived** at the pin.

> "**Arriving** sends a notification to the rider so they start
> walking down. **Arrived** starts a 5-minute wait clock. After
> those 5 minutes you can cancel as a no-show without affecting
> your rating — but the polite thing is to call first."

Pick up the rider. Tap **Start trip**.

> "Tapping Start trip locks in the route. From here, the rider can't
> change the destination — they'd have to cancel and book again.
> Keep the phone visible but don't tap anything until you arrive."

### 4. Completing the ride (3 min)

Arrive at the destination. Tap **Complete trip**.

> "The app shows you the final fare, the distance covered, the
> duration, and the rider's preferred payment method. For the pilot
> everything is cash. Collect the cash from the rider, give them
> change if needed, then tap **Confirm received**."

Driver watches the trainer settle.

### 5. After the ride (2 min)

> "Once you confirm payment, the app drops you back to the home
> screen, still **Online**. You'll be matched again as soon as
> another rider books nearby."

> "You'll see your daily earnings card update — that's gross. We take
> our 15% commission off the top automatically. You'll see the net
> in the payouts page."

> "We pay weekly, every Monday, to the bank account you gave us."

### 6. Cancellations (4 min)

> "Sometimes you'll need to cancel — flat tire, wrong vehicle, rider
> unreachable. Tap the menu, then **Cancel ride**, then pick the
> reason. Cancellation reasons matter: 'rider unreachable' after
> a 5-minute wait is fine, 'wrong vehicle' counts against you."

> "If you cancel more than 20% of accepted offers in any 50-ride
> window, we'll call you and figure out what's wrong. Three calls
> like that and we'll need to off-board you."

> "If the rider cancels on you, you'll see a notification. Your
> rating isn't affected. If they cancel after you've already arrived,
> we'll pay you the standard pickup fee."

### 7. Safety basics (4 min)

Read out the safety section of `ride-safety-checklist.md` (printed,
attached at the end of this guide). Specifically:

- Helmet for you AND the rider, every ride, no exceptions.
- Reflective vest while driving.
- Don't carry more than one passenger.
- Don't carry passengers under 12.
- If a rider seems intoxicated to the point of being unsafe — don't
  start the trip. Call dispatch.
- If you witness or are involved in an accident — call 112 first,
  then us. We have a hotline number on the laminated card.

### 8. Test ride (5 min)

Driver does steps 2-5 themselves on their own phone, with the
trainer riding shotgun (or following on a scooter). Ops shadow rider
books the trip.

Trainer ticks the items in `driver-approval-checklist.md` § "Training
ride" as the driver demonstrates each one.

If the driver completes the test ride correctly → sign off.
If anything went wrong → identify the gap, repeat from the relevant
section, re-run the test ride.

After two failed attempts → escalate to Ops lead.

### 9. Q&A (3 min)

> "Any questions? Anything I went too fast on?"

Capture the questions verbatim in the driver's notes in admin so we
can fold them into v2 of this guide.
